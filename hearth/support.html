Hearth Support

Hearth controls Napoleon® gas fireplaces that have eFIRE Bluetooth control: one-tap presets, widgets, Control Center, NFC tags, a sleep timer, and a running tally of what your fireplace costs to run.

For help, questions, or feedback, email support@mightypickle.dev and we'll get back to you within one business day.

Common questions:

Hearth can't find my fireplace.
Close the Napoleon eFIRE app completely (swipe it away) — the fireplace talks to only one phone at a time. Then stand in the same room as the fireplace and try again.

What's the Bluetooth password?
It's 0000 unless you changed it in the Napoleon app. You can update it in Hearth under Settings → Connection.

The fireplace didn't do what I asked.
If the handheld remote was used recently, it takes priority over Bluetooth. Switch the remote to OFF, then use Hearth again.

How is the cost calculated?
Hearth multiplies the hours your fireplace burned by its maximum rated gas input and the price you enter from your gas bill. Lower flame settings use somewhat less gas, so the figure is a ceiling.

Does my fireplace keep its own usage history?
No. Hearth logs every fire it lights or finds burning. For fires lit with the remote, add the hours on the Usage tab.

Which fireplaces work?
Napoleon fireplaces and inserts with the eFIRE Bluetooth module, including Elevation X, Altitude X, Ascent, Vector, Luxuria, Tall Linear Vector and Oakville models. If yours isn't listed in setup, choose Other and turn on the features it has.
