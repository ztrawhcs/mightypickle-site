Hearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

No data collected

Hearth collects no personal information. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising.

Bluetooth

Hearth uses Bluetooth only to talk directly to your fireplace. Commands go from your iPhone to the fireplace and nowhere else.

Your settings and usage log

Your presets, fireplace settings, gas price, and the log of when your fireplace burned are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Hearth is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see payment information.

Contact

Questions? Email support@mightypickle.dev.
